# Solar power

Silicon solar cells convert about 20 percent of sunlight into electricity. Perovskite cells are a
newer technology that has passed 25 percent efficiency in the lab, but they degrade faster than
silicon outdoors.

Panels produce direct current, so an inverter converts it to alternating current for the home.
A typical residential system costs between 2.50 and 3.50 US dollars per watt before incentives.
